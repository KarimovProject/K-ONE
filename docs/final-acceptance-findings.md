Severity guide:

- **BLOCKER:** the business flow cannot be completed, data is lost, or permission/security fails.

- **MEDIUM:** confusing use, translation, accessibility or responsive-layout problem.
- **LOW:** visual polish, spacing or motion issue that does not block work.
