# Product

## Register

product

## Users

International Department administrators, responsible employees, management approvers,
leadership viewers, content managers, and reception operators. They work in an operational
office context and need fast, dependable access to shared event master data with permissions
appropriate to their responsibilities.

## Product Purpose

IEMS provides one trusted workspace for planning and managing international events. Success
means staff can maintain clean reusable master data, understand system state quickly, and
complete routine tasks without ambiguity while confidential and public information remain
strictly separated.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, international. The interface should feel credible in a medical and corporate
environment while retaining enough color to make status and navigation immediately legible.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic consumer SaaS styling, decorative dashboard clutter, dense legacy admin grids,
ambiguous icon-only actions, excessive animation, and interfaces that imply unfinished
integrations are operational.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next operational action obvious.
2. Use familiar enterprise patterns and consistent component behavior.
3. Separate editable master data from future event workflows.
4. Communicate permissions and system state honestly.
5. Keep multilingual content readable and resilient at every supported viewport.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, associated field errors, meaningful labels,
screen-reader semantics, strong contrast, touch targets, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. The
interface must remain usable in Uzbek, Russian, and English without relying on color alone.
